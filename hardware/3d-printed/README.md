## Corner Brackets for LED Lightbox

**Design:** Corner brackets for building a photography lightbox frame  
**Published on Printables:** https://www.printables.com/model/1341411-corner-brackets-for-diy-photography-lightbox

### Purpose

These corner brackets are designed to build a cube-shaped LED lightbox that surrounds the Manège turntable system. The lightbox provides even, diffused lighting for professional product photography.

### Specifications

- **Wood Frame Size:** 20mm × 36mm (rectangular profile)
- **Bracket Type:** Corner connectors (8 pieces for a cube)
- **Material:** PLA or PETG recommended
- **Print Settings:** See Printables page for details

### How It Works

1. **Frame Construction:**
   - Cut 12 pieces of 20×36mm wood (4 per face × 3 faces)
   - Use 8 corner brackets to connect the frame into a cube
   - Manège turntable sits in the center

2. **Lighting Integration:**
   - Mount LED strips inside the frame
   - Cover with diffusion material (tracing paper, diffusion fabric)
   - Creates soft, even lighting from all sides

3. **Photography Benefits:**
   - 360° even illumination
   - Eliminates harsh shadows
   - Professional product photography quality
   - Compact and portable

### Assembly

![Lightbox Frame Assembly](lightbox-frame-assembly.png)

The brackets hold the wood frame at corners, creating a stable cube structure.

**Cube dimensions depend on wood length:**
- Example: 500mm wood pieces → 500mm cube interior
- Adjust size based on your product photography needs
- Larger for bigger objects, smaller for compact setup

### Bill of Materials

**For complete lightbox:**
- 8× Corner brackets (3D printed)
- 12× Wood pieces (20×36mm, length depends on desired size)
- LED strips (optional - for lighting)
- Diffusion material (tracing paper, fabric)
- Wood screws or glue (for assembly)

### Print Settings Recommendation

- **Layer Height:** 0.2mm
- **Infill:** 20-30%
- **Supports:** Not required (designed to print without)
- **Material:** PLA (indoor use) or PETG (more durable)
- **Quantity:** Print 8 brackets for one cube

### Integration with Manège

**Setup:**
1. Build lightbox cube using brackets and wood
2. Place Manège turntable in center
3. Position camera(s) outside one open face
4. Add LED strips and diffusion for lighting
5. Rotate products while camera captures from fixed position

**Benefits:**
- Consistent lighting across 360° rotation
- Professional white/neutral background
- Eliminates external light variations
- Creates studio-quality product photos

## Tips for 3D Printing Manège Parts

**General Guidelines:**

- **Material:** PLA for static parts, PETG for mechanical stress
- **Layer Height:** 0.2mm for most parts, 0.1mm for precision fits
- **Infill:** 20% for brackets, 50%+ for structural parts
- **Orientation:** Print for strength (layer lines perpendicular to stress)

**Design Considerations:**

- Account for wood tolerances (±0.5mm typical)
- Add clearance for easy assembly
- Consider vibration from motor (secure mounting)
- Plan for cable routing
